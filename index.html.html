The Six-Tusked Elephant

A storyboard of the Chhaddanta Jataka, where hatred meets a compassion so great it gives away its own tusks.

Begin the story
✿ ❀ ✿

Long ago, in the Himalayan forest beside Lake Chhaddanta, lived a king who was not a man. Follow his story in eight panels.

The Message

Hatred is answered, not by hatred, but by compassion. The Bodhisattva elephant gave up what was most precious to him, and in doing so rose above the anger that had hurt him.

About the source

The Chhaddanta Jataka is one of the tales of the Buddha’s earlier lives. A version of it is painted at Ajanta, where the murals use stories like this to teach compassion, forgiveness and generosity. Details vary between texts and between paintings, so this storyboard is an interpretation, not a copy of one mural.
